What is Food Safety?

Updated: March 31, 2026 | Reading time: 5 min

Food safety is the practice of handling, preparing, and storing food in ways that prevent foodborne illness and ensure safe consumption. Professionals in the field protect consumers, ensure compliance, and maintain food quality from farm to fork.

Types of Food Safety Hazards

Updated: March 31, 2026 | Reading time: 5 min

Food hazards are classified as biological, chemical, or physical. Understanding them helps food safety professionals control risks, prevent contamination, and ensure safe food production.

Updated: March 31, 2026 | Reading time: 5 min
