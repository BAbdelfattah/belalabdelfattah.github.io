About Me

Food Safety & HACCP Coach helping professionals and companies operate with confidence in real-world conditions.

Who I Help

I help food safety professionals and companies in Egypt build practical HACCP systems, pass inspections, and operate effectively in real environments.

My Experience

Over the past few years, I’ve worked across food factories, warehouses, and operational environments. I’ve seen how systems often look perfect on paper—but break under real pressure.

From audits to production challenges, I’ve been in situations where decisions had to be made quickly, balancing safety, compliance, and operations.

My Approach

My approach is simple: food safety must work in real operations—not just documentation.

  • Practical HACCP implementation
  • Real-world problem solving
  • Systems that teams can actually follow
  • Preparation for real audits—not theoretical ones

What I Do

  • 1:1 Coaching for HACCP systems
  • Audit preparation and readiness
  • Food safety system development
  • Career mentorship in food safety

From the Field

I’ve seen real cases where small issues—like damaged cans, poor handling, or weak processes—turned into serious operational risks.

These are the moments where food safety decisions matter most. My work focuses on helping professionals handle these situations with clarity and confidence.
